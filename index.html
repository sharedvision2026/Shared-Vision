What happens at the van

The van is a mobile eye clinic called Scheie in MOTION.

  1. 1

    Walk up to the van

    You don’t need an appointment, and the screening is free.

  2. 2

    Get your eyes checked

    The van team checks how well you see and how healthy your eyes are.

  3. 3

    Hear your results

    The van team tells you what the checks show.

  4. 4

    Get the care you need

    We help you get glasses or see an eye doctor if you need to.

See all 7 checks

Help after the screening

  • Free or low-cost glasses

    We hand out free reading glasses. For prescription glasses, we help you apply to a free or low-cost program.

  • Help with insurance

    We help you sign up for insurance, or use the insurance you have.

  • A ride to your eye appointment

    We help you get to your eye appointment with a SEPTA pass.
