What the van checks

  1. How well you see

    (visual acuity and refraction)

  2. Eye pressure

    This is a check for glaucoma.

    (intraocular pressure)

  3. How thick the clear front of your eye is

    (pachymetry)

  4. The angle between the clear front of your eye and the colored part

    This is another check for glaucoma.

    (anterior chamber angle)

  5. The layer of nerves at the back of your eye

    The van measures how thick it is, to see how healthy the back of your eye is.

    (retinal nerve fiber layer thickness)

  6. Your side vision

    (perimetry)

  7. A picture of the back of your eye

    (fundus imaging)

Free or low-cost glasses

We hand out free reading glasses. For prescription glasses, we help you apply to a free or low-cost program.

  • New Eyes

    New Eyes gives a basic pair of glasses to people with low income. You need a glasses prescription from the last two years. There is a fee to apply, and you don’t get it back.

  • ReSpectacle

    You find donated glasses that match your prescription and order them online. You pay a small fee for shipping.

  • Prevent Blindness: help paying for eye care

    A list of groups that help pay for eye exams, glasses and eye medicine, with their phone numbers.

Help with insurance and rides

  • Insurance

    We help you sign up for insurance, or use the insurance you have.

  • A ride to your eye appointment

    We help you get to your eye appointment with a SEPTA pass.

  • Eye doctor visits

    We help you set up the eye appointments you need.
