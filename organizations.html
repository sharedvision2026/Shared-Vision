Bring the van to your site

The Scheie in MOTION eye van can park at your shelter, church or program.

Email us to plan a visit

Or write to sharedvision2026@gmail.com

You can also ask the van team yourself

Room to park

The van needs a parking lot or street parking near your building. It takes up 2 to 3 SUV-sized spots.

It is 26 feet long and 11.5 feet tall.

What a visit looks like

The van can check the eyes of up to 25 people in a day.

  1. 1

    People walk up

    No one needs an appointment, and the screening is free.

  2. 2

    Their eyes get checked

    Three or four people on the van team run its six stations.

  3. 3

    They get their results

    An eye doctor joins by video, so each person hears what the checks found before they leave.

  4. 4

    Help after the visit

    If someone needs more care, we help with insurance, free or low-cost glasses and a ride to their eye appointment.

Other help for your clients

  • Service Link

    Student volunteers help your clients sign up for health insurance and find food, medicine, rides and housing.

  • Shelter Health Outreach Program

    Students visit Philadelphia shelters and soup kitchens every week. They check blood pressure, blood sugar and eyesight, and give vaccines.

  • Eagles Eye Mobile

    It gives free eye exams and glasses to children in Philadelphia public schools. Ask about a visit on the foundation’s contact form.

  • Eyes on Wheels in Pittsburgh

    Students run this mobile eye clinic. It gives free, full eye exams every month.
